About

Who I am.

I'm a sophomore at Georgia Tech studying Computer Science with concentrations in Artificial Intelligence and Information Internetworks. I care about building software that actually solves something — not just demos that look good in a pitch deck.

This past summer I interned at Google through Break Through Tech, where I shipped end-to-end features for a conversational AI travel app built on multi-agent systems, BigQuery, and Vertex AI. Before that I spent a semester doing research on sparse tensor benchmarks — the kind of work that makes you appreciate both mathematical elegance and stubborn performance realities.

Outside of code, I write about tech policy and co-authored a peer-reviewed memo on AI-enhanced disinformation threats to critical infrastructure, accepted to the Journal of Science Policy & Governance. I fold origami when I need to think, and I hike whenever Atlanta's trails will have me.

I'm looking for Summer 2027 roles in software engineering, AI/ML engineering, or research.
